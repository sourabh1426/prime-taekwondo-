🥋 The idea

What is Taekwondo?

Taekwondo is a Korean martial art and sport. It trains students in kicking-focused techniques, hand-based defence and attack, and self-control. Training develops the body and mind, and can support self-defence, personal growth and sport.

🎯 Main disciplines

How Taekwondo is practised

Students build strong basics first, then practise set forms and controlled competition skills with their coach.

Poomsae · FormsPlanned movements that practise balance, posture, focus and technique.
Kyorugi · SparringRule-based, controlled matches that use timing, distance and safe protective gear.
Core skillsFootwork, kicks, blocks, hand techniques, flexibility and coordination.
Train safelyWarm up, follow coach instructions, and practise contact skills only under supervision.
